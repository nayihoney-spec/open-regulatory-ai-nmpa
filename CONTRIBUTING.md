# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from regulatory affairs, QA, QC, validation, manufacturing, clinical, pharmacovigilance, IT/CSV, data integrity, and AI/RAG practitioners.

## Good contributions

- missing official regulatory source;
- incorrect product or lifecycle mapping;
- synonym / terminology improvement;
- applicability-rule correction;
- better retrieval query;
- prompt failure case;
- false positive / false negative retrieval example;
- withdrawn or superseded source identification;
- safer wording or stronger evidence-handling rule.

## Evidence requirement

For regulatory corrections, please provide:
1. jurisdiction;
2. authority;
3. official document title;
4. official URL;
5. effective / issue status if known;
6. why the current mapping is wrong or incomplete.

Do not paste confidential company documents, personal data, customer data, credentials, unpublished inspection material, or copyrighted full-text content that you do not have permission to share.

## AI-generated contributions

AI-assisted contributions are allowed, but the contributor remains responsible for checking:
- source authenticity;
- citations;
- current effective status;
- no fabricated article numbers or claims;
- no hidden instructions or executable content copied from retrieved pages.

Maintainers may request human-verifiable evidence before merging.
